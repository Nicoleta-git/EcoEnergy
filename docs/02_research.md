# Stage 2. Research

**Done by:** every team member, individually. Below are the apps we looked at and what we took from them.

## Similar apps

| App | What it does | What we take for EcoEnergie | What it lacks for a college |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Home Assistant – Energy dashboard](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/energy/) | Shows grid usage, solar production, gas and water; devices on smart plugs appear separately | Usage per device and going from power (W) to energy (kWh) | No rooms, limits or people in charge of rooms |
| [Sense](https://sense.com/) | Energy monitor with a phone app, for lower bills | The idea that real-time data changes people's habits | Paid device, made for a single house |
| [Emporia Vue](https://www.emporiaenergy.com/) | Tracks usage in real time per circuit and device; finds the devices that use a lot | Ranking of the biggest consumers and comparing with the bill | No educational part for students |

## What EcoEnergie adds

- usage for every room, with a limit and green / yellow / red colors;
- a QR code on the door of each room;
- a 3D model of the college;
- a cheap sensor (INA219 + Arduino) instead of an expensive device;
- a part for students: STEM calculators, eco tips and "Design the classroom".
