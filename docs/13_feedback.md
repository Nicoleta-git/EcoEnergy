# Stage 13. Collecting feedback

**Done by:** Nicoleta

We show the mockup to a classmate from another team and to the teacher, let them use it on their own and write down what they say.

## What we ask them to do

1. Log in with a test account.
2. Find the room that uses the most today.
3. Open a room page and its QR code.
4. Enter a new meter reading.
5. Find the usage for the last 30 days.
