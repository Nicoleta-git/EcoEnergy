# Stage 14. Improving the product

**Done by:** Faina

What we changed from the first version until now.

| Before | What we changed | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark blue theme, like every other app | Almost black background, green for actions, gradient buttons | To match the energy topic and look cleaner |
| A logo with a bolt in a square | The "ECO ⚡ ENERGIE" logo, with the bolt between the words | The logo should say the app's name |
| Very rounded corners | 3 px corners | More discreet |
| A "Normal" label on every room | The label only shows on yellow and red rooms | The rooms with problems stand out right away |
| Long texts under the titles | Short, one-sentence texts | Faster to read |
| A separate alerts page | Removed; going over the limit shows directly through the color | It was one more screen to learn; the colors say the same thing |
| Sensor over Wi-Fi too (ESP32) | Only Arduino over USB | Easier to build and to explain |
| Statistics with hard formulas | Average, minimum and maximum | Clear without explanations |
| Didn't exist | The "Design the classroom" page with missions and notes | The part for students |
