## The problem we noticed

Every month the college pays one electricity bill for the whole building, but nobody knows how much each room uses or when energy is wasted. Without this data the waste can't be seen, so it can't be reduced.

## Examples from the college

- Lights stay on in empty rooms, during breaks and after the last class.
- The computers in the IT labs stay on or in standby overnight and at weekends.
- The projector stays on after the lesson ends.
- (to be filled in by the team: one more example we saw, with the room and the day)

## Why it is a problem

- **High costs:** the college pays the bill but doesn't know where the money goes.
- **The waste is invisible:** without data you can't show where energy is lost.
- **Nobody is responsible:** the person in charge of a room can't see how much it uses.
- **The environment:** every wasted kWh also means more CO₂.

## How it is done now

The consumption is only on the monthly bill for the whole building. There is no record per room, per day or per device.

## Who is affected

| Who | How |
| --- | --- |
| The college administration | Pays big bills without knowing why |
| The people in charge of rooms | Have no data about their room |
| Students and teachers | Don't see the waste and don't learn how to save energy |

## What we need

A simple program where the consumption is recorded per room, shown in charts and colored differently when it goes over a limit.
